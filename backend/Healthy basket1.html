How to play

Healthy food (apple, banana, orange, strawberry, grapes, pineapple, carrot, broccoli, avocado, chicken leg, spinach) = points & gold coins. Missing a fruit drops 1 life!

⚡ Super Boost Star: Catch it to trigger 10 seconds of Automatic Auto-Play (Unhealthy food is shielded)!

Junk food (pizza, burger, fries, donut, soda, coca cola) = lose one heart.

Move with mouse / touch / ← → keys / On-Screen Manual Buttons. Space = pause.
